Don't neglect a termite problem

Left untreated, termites quietly destroy the wood in your home or commercial property and build their colonies inside it. Before you know it, they've multiplied — and so has the damage.

Our licensed termite inspectors examine your property thoroughly, from the crawl space to the attic. We don't just glance around: we probe wood, check behind insulation, and follow the evidence. After the evaluation, you get a clear termite treatment program and a free estimate.

What we look for

  • Mud tubes on foundations and framing
  • Hollow or damaged wood
  • Pellets at windows, walls, and doors
  • Swarmers and discarded wings
  • Moisture conditions termites love
  • Activity hidden behind insulation

Know your enemy

The different kinds of termites

Different termites leave different clues. Our inspectors know exactly where each kind hides — and drywood and dampwood termites don't even need ground contact to reach your home.

Subterranean termites

The most destructive by far. They nest in soil and build mud tunnels up into your framing, eating away at wooden structures from the inside out.

Drywood termites

They live entirely inside the wood, thriving in warm areas like attics. Small pellets at the base of windows, walls, or doors are the telltale sign.

Dampwood termites

Larger termites that live in small colonies and feed on moist wood — framing kept damp by leaking gutters, or wood behind stucco and around masonry porches and stairs.
