Expert termite control services

Inspect first. Then eliminate.

Trust the professionals at United Termite & Pest Control to conduct a thorough inspection of your property to check for termites. Once we know exactly what we're dealing with, we deliver targeted treatments to control and eliminate them.

We've been doing this since 1998 — and we never stop learning. Our team attends yearly seminars to stay up to date with the latest termite control technologies, so your home gets today's methods, not last decade's. Depending on your property, we treat with liquid Termidor, the Advance Termite Bait System, or both.

Advance Termite Bait System (ATBS)

We use the Advance Termite Bait System to eliminate termites from your property. Discreet stations placed around your home's perimeter intercept foraging termites, which carry the bait back and share it with the colony — taking out the source of the problem, not just the visitors.

  • Targets the colony — bait spreads termite to termite, back to the nest
  • Low-impact — stations install without disturbing finished landscaping
  • Ongoing protection — stations keep guarding your home after the colony is gone
